What Helix does,and which domain owns it.

Aya employment types covered: Travel, Per Diem, WFD, Locums, Perm, and Local Long Term. IRP is Per Diem × client-employed; its records are built now, its workflows later. Interim Leadership, Education, and Government are not unique employment types in Helix: they map to Travel with different work settings. Not scoped: Access Control; small enough to leave out for now.
