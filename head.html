What Helix does,and which domain owns it.

Helix is Aya. Helix is its own platform beside Aya’s systems (CoreAPI, Nova), but it operates as Aya: Aya stays the employer of record, holds the client contracts, bills clients, and pays talent, so a Helix placement looks like any other Aya placement to a client or a clinician. Helix reaches Aya’s systems only through Integration Hub, and is expected to replace them over time, not soon. Scoped in: Travel, Per Diem, WFD, Locums, Perm, and Local Long Term; IRP is Per Diem × client-employed. Interim Leadership and Education map to Travel (Education is the School work setting); Government is a client sector on the worksite, not a type. Scoped out for now: see the Not modeled view.
